Make THE DEX HALL from the sheet, with one big exception: this is the building only.
Do NOT model the open book on the roof, its carved stand, its ribbon, or the flying pages. Do NOT model the two book lecterns. Those are existing models already in the game and will be placed on this building afterwards, so they must not be in this file.
Leave room for them: keep the middle of the roof flat at Y = 13.71, at least 17 wide and 12 deep and centred 4 units forward of the middle, finished with shingles and trim, for the book to sit on. Build the two empty stone pads out front and nothing on them, each 7 wide, 6 deep and 1.2 high, centred 14 units either side of the middle and 15 units forward of the middle.
Everything else on the sheet is included: purple shingle roof, gold corners and ridge, planks and timber framing, stone foundation, carved columns, gable with gold book emblem, windows with book-stacked sills, bookshelves through the doorway, iron lanterns, purple banners, carved steps with carpet and gold rods, and the small stacks of books by the door.
