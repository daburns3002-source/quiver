THE ARCANIST'S SET, a wizard in blues and gold:
- GIANT STAR HAT on the head: a very wide dark-blue flat brim, a gold band, then a bright blue stepped cone of four shrinking tiers that droops backwards and to one side, with a pale gold star gem on the front of the band. Under the brim a dark-blue cowl covers the back and sides of the head, open at the face.
- ROBE on the chest: bright blue, a gold stripe down the middle of the front and a square pale-gold gem on it.
- CRYSTAL SHOULDERS: a dark-blue block on each shoulder with a glowing cyan crystal growing straight up out of it, stepped to a pale tip.
- SASH: a gold sash round the waist over the robe.
- ARMS: blue sleeves on the upper arms, wide blue bell sleeves with a thick gold cuff on the forearms. Bare hands.
- LEGS: the robe's skirt, widening in two stepped tiers down to a thick gold hem above the ankle; small navy shoes under it. The skirt is split down the middle into a left and a right half, one per leg.
- No cape.
