THE WARDEN'S SET, a knight in steel, red and gold:
- HORNED HELM on the head: pale steel dome with a brighter top plate, a darker brow band above the eyes, a cheek guard down each side of the face, a back plate over the neck, a stepped GOLD horn sticking out and up on each side, and a RED plume crest running front to back along the top with a short tail behind. Open at the face.
- BREASTPLATE on the chest: pale steel, with a RED tabard down the middle of the front and a square gold crest on it.
- HUGE SHOULDER PLATES: a big steel block on each shoulder, wider than the arm, with a brighter stepped top and a square gold stud on its front.
- RED CAPE down the back: two tiers, the lower one wider and further out, ending at mid-thigh with a darker red hem.
- BELT: a thick gold belt, a dark steel skirt of plates under it, and the red tabard's tail hanging over the front.
- ARMS: dark steel on the upper arms, pale steel vambraces with a bright elbow guard on the forearms. Bare hands.
- LEGS: pale steel thigh plates, dark steel greaves with a pale knee plate on the front, dark steel boots with a pale toe cap.
