Area: DROWNED VILLAGE (sea blues and teals, shallow water, bronze, barnacles, gold). The three panels, left to right:
1 RARE "Bubble Stream": a pale sky-blue ribbon with small pale-blue square bubble blocks with white highlights rising off it.
2 EPIC "Splash Wake": a white-foam and sea-blue ribbon with splashing square water-spray cubes flicking out to its sides.
3 LEGENDARY "Tide Bell", from Old Tollcrab the bell-shelled crab: a GLOWING sea-green and teal ribbon edged in gold, with gold sparkles and bright water droplets along it; it clearly glows and lights the floor around it.
