1 RARE "Leaf Drift": a soft fresh-green ribbon with small square green and yellow-green leaves tumbling off it.
2 EPIC "Firefly Wake": a dusky deep-green ribbon dotted with small blinking warm-yellow firefly cubes that drift up and away from it.
3 LEGENDARY "Royal Goo", from King Bloblin the slime king: a thick GLOWING lime-green slime ribbon, translucent like jelly, with goo cubes dripping off it and small gold crown-sparkles twinkling along it; it clearly glows and lights the floor around it.
