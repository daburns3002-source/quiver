These are three options for the game's FIRST SEASON trail, a Legendary given only on the paid track of the "Starwish" season, whose theme is toy wishing-star charms: pastel pink and mint, cream white, gold stars, flat ribbon wraps and glowing star gems (see the attached weapon set). Each option must GLOW softly, light the floor a little around it, and look like a wish come true: sweet, bright and special, but clearly NOT the game's Mythic trail, which is a deep night-blue ribbon full of white plus-shaped stars, so none of these is night-blue or full of white stars. The floor in all three panels is a plain soft dusk-lilac stone floor. The three panels, left to right:
A "Wish Ribbon": a flat glowing ribbon in two side-by-side bands, pastel pink and mint, with a thin cream edge, like a gift ribbon unrolling behind the hero, and small gold PLUS-shaped star charms (perfect crosses, not round) bobbing up off it and settling.
B "Star Gem Wake": a glowing cream-white ribbon with a pastel pink core, scattered with small faceted pink and mint star gems (cube-cut, four-pointed) that tumble off it and twinkle gold.
C "Wishing Comet": a glowing gold ribbon that fades from warm gold to pastel pink and then mint along its length, with chunky cream and gold four-pointed star blocks drifting up off it and blinking.
