A clean game-art reference sheet, 16:9, light grey background, titled "Drowned
Village pet attack pieces". It shows the projectiles and small effects for five
flooded-village pets' attacks in Arrow Legends, a Roblox top-down archer game.
The attached images are the five pets themselves — Abyss Jelly, Buoy Puffer,
Inkpot, Slate Turtle, Tide Wilf — and every piece below must take its colours
from the pet it belongs to.

Style: blocky, low-poly, like Roblox parts and Pet Simulator: every piece built
from a few chunky bevelled cubes and slabs, flat colours, no outlines, no
gradients except a soft glow where noted. Simple and readable from a top-down
camera. Small: each piece is about the size of the pet's head or smaller.

These are water pieces and must not read as forest ones. No leaves, no petals,
no spores, no wood, no fire. Their language is water, ink, deep-sea glow, wet
stone and the turquoise of a flooded street.

Lay out seven panels, four across and three down, the last two cells left
empty. Each panel: the piece large in three-quarter view, a small side view,
and a strip of three small frames showing its motion. Label each panel with its
name in plain blocky letters.

1. Tentacle Sting (Abyss Jelly): a trailing tentacle of four or five small
   cubes strung in a line, thinning toward the tip, each cube a translucent
   deep violet-blue with a soft cyan glow. It lashes straight out from the pet
   and recoils in a curl; frames: coiled, lashed out straight, curling back.
   NOT a tongue — the segments hang loose and trail behind the tip.

2. Accord Mark (Abyss Jelly): the mark its canopy lays on three enemies at once.
   A flat ring of small translucent cyan cubes lying on the ground, with a
   single glowing violet cube hanging just above its centre. Show three of them
   side by side, joined by a thin line of tiny cyan cubes running between the
   rings — the blow shared among all three. Frames: the ring settling, the link
   lighting up, the ring fading.

3. Spine Burst (Buoy Puffer): a ring of short, blunt cream spine wedges thrown
   outward flat along the ground from under the pet, over a low pale turquoise
   water ring. Frames: spines drawn in tight, bursting out, the water ring
   spreading on alone. NOT a crown, NOT a shockwave of light — these are
   physical spines over water.

4. Ink Squirt (Inkpot): a blob of near-black ink, two or three cubes with a
   deep indigo sheen, flying in a flat arc with a short trail of smaller ink
   cubes behind it. Frames: fired, flying, bursting.

5. Ink Cloud (Inkpot): what the squirt leaves. A low flat patch of near-black
   ink tiles on the ground with a loose cloud of dark indigo cubes hanging
   above it, thick enough to hide what is inside — this is a blinding cloud,
   not a healing patch. Frames: bursting outward, hanging full, thinning away.

6. Slate Shelter (Slate Turtle): a low dome over the player built from flat
   grey-green slate slabs laid like overlapping shell plates, translucent, with
   wet green algae edging each plate and a pale turquoise rim where it meets
   the ground. Frames: the plates rising and locking together, holding, sinking
   away. NOT a howl, NOT rings of sound — this is a shell.

7. Tide Slash (Tide Wilf): a crescent arc of flat turquoise water slabs, darker
   at the leading edge, with a scatter of small white foam cubes flung off its
   outer rim. Frames: the arc starting, full sweep, breaking into foam. NOT a
   leaf crescent — this is a sheet of thrown water.
