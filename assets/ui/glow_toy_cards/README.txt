Arrow Legends upgrade cards, Glow Toy style.

icons/ - 512x512 icon on its rarity tile, one per upgrade.
icons_transparent/ - 512x512 icon only, transparent background, for drawing on your own tile.
banners/ - 984x558 banner with the skill name, in the upgrade's rarity.
banners_blank/ - one blank banner per rarity, for live TextLabels.
tiles_blank/ - one blank icon tile per rarity.
cards/ - blank crystal card body per rarity, plus the Mythic card back.
data/UpgradeCards.lua - ModuleScript table of every upgrade. Paste asset ids after uploading.
data/upgrades.json - same data as JSON.

All images keep their padding so glows and sparkles are not cropped; anchor banners and cards at their centre.
Stats are placeholders.
