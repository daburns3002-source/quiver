# Pets — Drowned Village (Act 2, area 3)

The third egg's pet pool. **Design only; nothing here is built; the owner
picks.** Ideas by Codex (2026-10-27), from the owner's ask: "ok make pets
and weapons for the 3rd area". The meta loop is `PROGRESSION.md` ("Pets"),
the area is `WORLD.md` (3, Act 2 · The Lowlands), and its enemies and arenas
are in `ENEMIES-AND-ARENAS.md` ("3. Drowned Village"). This follows
`PETS-THORNWOOD.md`'s pool, tables and rarity ladder.

**Style: blocky**, exactly as `PETS-FOREST.md`: a few chunky bevelled boxes
like Roblox parts, a big cube head as big as the body or bigger, a box body,
short block legs, fins, flippers and tentacles as flat slabs and wedges,
flat colours, and a flat face decal with two big shiny eyes and a small
mouth. Aquatic pets paddle just above the ground; they do not need actual
water to follow you. Only the Mythic has a conspicuous magical float.

**Look.** Shallow turquoise water, grey cobbles, ruined plank houses with
blue slate roofs, orange lanterns, rope fences, docks, barrels, crates,
reeds and the stone well. Colours come from
`assets/concepts/blocky/arenas/area03_drowned_village.jpg` and the sheets in
`assets/concepts/blocky/drowned/`: water turquoise and teal, slate blue,
plank brown, lantern orange, moss green and foam white. Old Tollcrab adds
coral red, a big teal bell, gold bands and cream flowers. Mist and gulls
give the village its quiet harbour feeling; these are living companions,
not ghosts or drowned corpses.

No earlier animal is repeated, apart from the required Wilf family: no
slime, acorn, frog, mushroom, snail, firefly, stump, fox, owl, fawn,
piglet/boar, hedgehog, bulb, mouse, mole, beetle, bat, bear, lynx, spider or
dragon. No goat or ram (Act 3's Cliff Goat). The crab is this area's boss
heir, as the Thorn Princess is the Broodmother's.

No sheets or models are made by this document. Future sheets would follow
area 1's layout: title and rarity badge; front, three-quarter front, right
side, back; action pose; in-game size beside a Roblox avatar; six-colour
palette. The egg sheet would show egg, stand, hatch and Golden variant.

## The egg

**Drowned Village Egg**: one egg at Drowned Village's stand in the lobby,
bought with the area's coins (`PROGRESSION.md`: one egg tier per area).
A chunky faceted turquoise egg with a foam-white lower half, two broad
slate-blue patches like roof tiles, a rope band and a little brass bell
clasp. A moss-green reed tuft sits beside the clasp; warm orange light
shows through three square shell seams. It reads as a harbour treasure,
not a second crab bell.

The **stand** is a short square plank dock over a turquoise water slab,
on mossy grey cobbled feet. Four rope-linked posts frame the egg; one
orange lantern hangs from the rear post. A small barrel and reed clump
sit at opposite corners, and the front carries a wooden nameboard reading
**DROWNED VILLAGE**. Keep the egg clear above the props from the game camera.
On the **hatch**, the rope band unknots, the bell gives one little toll,
and shell chunks and square foam droplets pop out in a turquoise splash.
All twelve pets hatch from it; rarity odds are not set here.

A **Golden Drowned Village Egg** would keep the same silhouette, with a
gold shell, antique-brass rope band, pearl-white seams and an amber glow.
Whether Golden eggs are separate eggs or only a look remains open, as in
areas 1–2.

## Rarity escalation

The same ladder as Mossy Glade and Thornwood; each tier adds something
visible from across the lobby:

| Rarity | What it adds |
|---|---|
| Common | flat colours, no glow |
| Uncommon | richer, more saturated colours and one accessory |
| Rare | one glowing part |
| Epic | glowing markings, a violet rim light, floating motes |
| Legendary | gold trim or a crown, a golden aura, gold sparkles |
| Mythic | floats; an iridescent rainbow sheen, a ring of light, orbiting motes |

Golden and Rainbow variants sit on top of any rarity with the silhouette
unchanged, as in area 1. Painted orange on a Common is not an emissive lamp.

## Attacks

One combat behaviour each, in the shape of `Config/PetAttacks`: a piece
fired on a cooldown. **Proposals, not built or approved.** Numbers are
starting points. Common through Epic Attack clips move only the pet's own
parts; gameplay pieces are separate, simple effects. Legendaries and the
Mythic add the more spectacular effect pieces, as in area 1.

| Pet | Rarity | Role | Attack |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dock Otter | Common | Shoots | **First Crack**: every 5 s claps a flat pebble at one reachable enemy; deals double its normal pet-hit damage if that enemy is still at full health, otherwise one ordinary hit |
| Reed Duck | Common | Tanks | **False Quack**: every 10 s quacks at the nearest ordinary enemy; its next aimed projectile targets the duck's current spot instead of you, then the duck waddles aside; lasts at most 2 s and does not redirect bosses or ground attacks |
| Foam Pup | Common | Shoots | **Double Splash**: every 6 s slaps one enemy's position with water, then repeats the same small splash at that exact ground position 0.8 s later; a moving enemy can escape the second hit |
| Slate Turtle | Uncommon | Tanks | **Roof Huddle**: every 14 s tucks in beside you for 3 s, reducing incoming damage by 20%; hits still land, and it neither blocks shots nor grants invulnerability |
| Reed Axolotl | Uncommon | Heals | **Fresh Rinse**: every 12 s, if you have a removable slow or poison, shakes off a clean-water splash that removes those current effects; no health restoration, no immunity to the next hazard tick, and no removal of hooks or scripted boss mechanics |
| Buoy Puffer | Rare | Shoots | **Payback Puff**: arms after its owner takes damage; at most once every 8 s inflates and fires one blunt spine at that damage's living, reachable enemy source for triple its ordinary pet-hit damage; environmental damage cannot arm it |
| Pearl Pony | Rare | Shoots | **Pearl Watch**: every 8 s places one pearl beside you for 4 s; it strikes the first enemy that enters its small radius, once, then disappears; at most one pearl, with no splash, slow or root |
| Mist Heron | Epic | Shoots | **Last Catch**: every 7 s pecks the lowest-health reachable enemy; an ordinary enemy already below 15% health is finished outright, otherwise it takes one normal pet hit; elites and bosses always take only the normal hit |
| Inkpot | Epic | Controls | **Ink Blink**: every 11 s squirts one enemy's face; for 3 s its aimed shots cannot acquire targets beyond 6 studs, though it can still move and attack close up; no slow, damage bonus or effect on bosses and untargeted area attacks |
| Tide Wilf | Legendary | Shoots | **Wake Chase**: every 10 s splashes a foam wake onto one reachable enemy for 3 s; each 3 studs that enemy travels triggers one little splash hit, up to four, then the wake ends; standing still takes no extra hits |
| Toll Prince | Legendary | Controls | **Quiet Toll**: every 12 s rings its bell and sends a gold-edged teal note at one enemy; for 2 s an ordinary enemy cannot begin a ranged attack, but can move and use melee; existing shots and committed tells finish, and bosses receive one normal pet hit instead |
| Abyss Jelly | Mythic | Shoots | **Deep Accord**: every 16 s joins up to three reachable enemies with a floating rainbow-edged water canopy for 4 s; direct player damage to one is divided equally among the surviving linked enemies instead of all landing on the struck enemy; one enemy takes the full amount, and the total damage is never multiplied |

These effects do not repeat areas 1–2's pet attacks: no burning bolts,
healing pickups or patches, loot collection, shot-blocking shell, roots,
slows, charging pet, homing volley, damage-vulnerability mark, summons,
beam, revive, bramble wall, web crossings or ricocheting wheel. The
heron's finish and otter's opening hit occupy opposite ends of a fight;
the duck redirects aim, whereas the crab prevents a new ranged attack.

For a future build: all targeting, damage, cleansing, redirects and
execution checks are server-decided, per owner and pet. Boss and elite
immunities are explicit. Wake Chase counts the target's own ordinary
movement, not teleports or forced displacement. Deep Accord transfers only
direct player damage after its normal calculation, without transferring
status effects or triggering another on-hit event; it cannot join a second
link group, and duplicate canopies do not stack. One canopy per pet, at
most three targets, ends on room change or run end. Decoys never move a
player, and no effect permits walking or firing through cover.

## The Tide Wilf's colours

Keep `PETS-WILFS.md`'s sea-teal and foam-white coat, fin ears and tail,
sailor neckerchief, anchor tag and splash. Its head and body are teal;
square muzzle, chest and paws are foam white. The family's identifying
**long white sock on its own front right leg** runs from foot to body;
the front left has only a short white paw. Fins are wedges on the usual
ears and two-slab tail, preserving the family silhouette. Slate-blue
neckerchief, brass-gold anchor tag, gold aura and sparkles make it read as
Legendary without turning its whole coat gold.

## The Mythic: Abyss Jelly

A living deep-sea jellyfish, with a friendly pearl face and the stillness
of a lantern far below the harbour. Its dark canopy contrasts with the
bright turquoise lower tiers. Bioluminescent teal seams outline the chunky
box shape; pearl squares orbit slowly and a thin rainbow ring sits below
it. During Deep Accord its four ribbons lift into the corners of a water
canopy, briefly joining the chosen targets with broad luminous ribbons.
No crown, antlers, dragon body or bramble frame: its spectacle is a quiet
piece of the deep sea coming to the surface.

## Open decisions (the owner's)

For the owner to pick: which pets and attacks to keep or rename, and which
go to 3D first. Tide Wilf, Toll Prince and Abyss Jelly show the area's
identity most strongly. The proposed cooldowns and numbers need balancing;
the Golden Drowned Village Egg remains a look unless a separate egg is
chosen. This document makes no hatch-odds or economy change.
