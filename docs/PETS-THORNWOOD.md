# Pets — Thornwood (Act 1, area 2)

The second egg's pet pool. **Design only; nothing here is built; the owner
picks.** The meta loop these pets live in is `PROGRESSION.md` ("Pets"), the
area is `WORLD.md` (area 2, bramble boars and thistles, boss the
Broodmother) and `ENEMIES-AND-ARENAS.md` ("2. Thornwood").

**Style: blocky**, exactly as `PETS-FOREST.md` sets it (owner, 2026-10-23):
a few chunky bevelled boxes like Roblox parts, a big cube head (as big as
the body or bigger), a box body, short block legs, ears, tails and wings as
flat slabs and wedges, flat colours, and the face a flat decal (two big
shiny eyes and a small mouth). Colours come from Thornwood itself
(`assets/concepts/blocky/arenas/area02_thornwood.jpg`): dark-green hedges,
plum-purple thorny vines with cream thorn tips, purple bramble bushes, lime
grass, brown dirt, mossy grey rock; and from its enemies
(`assets/concepts/blocky/enemies/act1/`: Bramble Boar, Bramble Sow and her
Boarlets, Burr Thistle, Bramble Lurker) and its boss
(`boss02_thornweaver_views.png`, the Broodmother).

Ideas and sheets by Codex (owner, 2026-10-26: "i need codex to create ideas
and reference sheets for pets and weapons for area 2 now"), reviewed
against area 1: no repeats of Mossy Glade's animals (no slime, acorn, frog,
mushroom, snail, firefly, stump, fox, owl or fawn), no crab (Old Tollcrab is
Act 2's boss), no ram or goat (the Cliff Goat is Act 3's), only one insect
(the owner's earlier all-insect pass "went too far"), and the only wolf is
the area's own Wilf.

Concept sheets (Codex, 2026-10-26) are in
`assets/concepts/blocky/pets/thornwood/`, one per pet in area 1's sheet
layout (`assets/concepts/pets/forest/`: title and rarity badge; front,
three-quarter front, right side, back; an action pose; in-game size next to
a Roblox avatar; six-colour palette), plus `13_thornwood_egg.png` (egg,
stand, hatch, golden egg) and `contact_sheet.png` (eleven pets, then a row
of the three Mythic options). The dropped Briarbloom's sheet is kept aside as
`options/12x_briarbloom_old_views.png`.

**Owner's changes (2026-10-26):** "the bramble wilf color scheme needs to
be fixed, the mythic pet needs to be something else." The Bramble Wilf is
recoloured (below), and the Briarbloom peacock is replaced by three Mythic
options for the owner to pick from (*The Mythic: three options*).

**Spiders.** Act 1's regular enemies have no spiders (owner, 2026-10-22);
the Broodmother's Thornlings are hers alone. The Thorn Princess is her heir,
the way the Slime Prince is the Slime King's, so she is the one spider here,
drawn as a cute baby with no fangs and no webs on her body (her webs appear
only as her attack, as the Broodmother's do).

## The egg

**Thornwood Egg**: one egg, at Thornwood's stand in the lobby
(`PROGRESSION.md`: one egg tier per area, bought with that area's coins). A
chunky faceted cream egg with dark-green bramble patches, two plum bramble
vine bands set with blunt cream thorns, and a cluster of red berries under
a purple leaf on top, in a nest of hedge blocks and purple bramble bushes.
The **stand** is a grassy plinth over mossy-grey rock and dirt, with a
wooden nameboard on its front, framed by vine-wrapped hedge blocks and
purple bramble bushes. On the **hatch** the bramble bands spring apart
and shell chunks, purple leaves and berries pop out. All twelve pets hatch
from it; the odds are not set here (`PROGRESSION.md` names the weights but
no numbers).

The sheet also shows a **Golden Thornwood Egg** (gold shell, antique-gold
vines, ruby berries). Whether it is a separate egg or only a look is *open*,
as for the Golden Mossy Egg.

## Rarity escalation

The same ladder as Mossy Glade (`PETS-FOREST.md`); each tier adds something
a player can see from across the lobby:

| Rarity | What it adds |
|---|---|
| Common | flat colours, no glow |
| Uncommon | richer, more saturated colours and one accessory |
| Rare | one glowing part (a horn, a berry) |
| Epic | glowing markings, a violet rim light, floating motes |
| Legendary | gold trim or a crown, a golden aura, gold sparkles |
| Mythic | floats; an iridescent rainbow sheen, a ring of light, orbiting motes |

Golden and Rainbow variants sit on top of any rarity with the silhouette
unchanged, as in area 1.

## Attacks

One combat behaviour each (`PROGRESSION.md`: shoots, heals, collects,
tanks), in the shape of `Config/PetAttacks` (a piece fired on a cooldown).
Proposed, not chosen: the owner picks, as for area 1 on 2026-10-24. Lower
tiers move only the pet's own parts in their Attack clip; the Legendaries
and the Mythic add effect pieces, as area 1's do.

| Pet | Rarity | Role | Attack |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bark Hoglet | Common | Shoots | **Little Tusk Rush**: every 5 s charges a short straight line into the nearest reachable enemy, bumping it back, then trots home |
| Burrball | Common | Controls | **Cling Burr**: every 4 s flicks a burr that sticks to an enemy and slows it for 2 s |
| Budbulb | Common | Shoots | **Thorn Pop**: every 3 s its bud opens and fires a thorn needle that pierces the first enemy into a second |
| Berry Mouse | Uncommon | Heals | **Berry Delivery**: every 18 s while you are hurt, tosses you a red berry that heals a little on arrival |
| Thistle Mole | Uncommon | Collects | **Pocket Shovels**: every 6 s burrows to a nearby dropped coin cluster and pops up beside you with it |
| Briar Beetle | Rare | Tanks | **Bark Bunker**: every 12 s plants itself in front of you for 3 s, its plates blocking up to three enemy shots |
| Berry Bat | Rare | Shoots | **Berry Bonk**: every 5 s drops a hard berry on one enemy that bursts into a small splash of damaging seeds |
| Bramble Bear | Epic | Controls | **Hedge Gate**: every 10 s slams its fists down to raise a short bramble barrier for 3 s that stops ordinary enemies (your shots pass; bosses push through) |
| Briar Lynx | Epic | Shoots | **Thorn Shears**: every 6 s crosses its claws to fling two thorn blades that hit one target from opposite sides |
| Bramble Wilf | Legendary | Shoots | **Briar Pounce**: every 8 s pounces on an enemy and a fan of gold-edged thorn needles bursts forward as it lands, then bounds home round cover |
| Thorn Princess | Legendary | Controls | **Princess Stitch**: every 10 s stitches three sparkling magenta web strands across a group of enemies for 3 s, hurting and slowing whatever crosses them |
| Thornwake | Mythic | Shoots | **Briar Wheel**: every 14 s its tail curls into a glowing thorn wheel that rolls off and ricochets between up to three enemies, one heavy hit each |

Thornwood's flavours are thorns, burrs that slow, web strands, bramble
walls and boar charges, so none repeats an area-1 attack. Two notes for
when they are built: Little Tusk Rush and Briar Pounce move the pet to an
enemy and back, like the Glade Fox's Leaf Dash, so they would use
`Shared/Util/PetDash` (round cover, never through it); Hedge Gate's barrier
must not block the player.

## Built (2026-10-26)

All twelve were built in Claude Design from their sheets, one agent each, compared view by view until they matched, and exported with their clips to `assets/models/pets/thornwood/` (Idle, a movement loop, Follow, a signature loop, Attack). They wait for the owner's Studio import (Merge Meshes off) before `tools/imports_to_luau.py` and `tools/glb_clips_to_luau.py` wire them in (`docs/PIPELINES.md` §10).
