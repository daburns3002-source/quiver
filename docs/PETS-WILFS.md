# Wilfs — one pet for every area

Design only; nothing here is built. Two owner rules (2026-10-23):

- **Every wolf in the game is a Wilf** (plural Wilfs): the Ember Wolf is the
  Ember Wilf, enemy and pet.
- **There is a Wilf pet for every area**: ten, one per area of `WORLD.md`.

Pets in general, the eggs and the rarity ladder are in `PROGRESSION.md` and
`PETS-FOREST.md`; this doc adds only the Wilfs.

## One family, ten coats

Every Wilf has the same blocky silhouette, the Ember Wilf's: a big cube head
with wedge ears, a square muzzle, a box body, four block legs and a tail of
two stacked slabs. Only colours, markings, one or two accessories and glow
change per area, so the ten read as a set to collect, and in 3D they can
share one model and one rig with a recoloured mesh and swapped accessories.
Style as in `PETS-FOREST.md`: blocky, after Pet Simulator and Steal an Egg,
copying nothing from them.

**The Wilf marking** (owner, 2026-10-23): every Wilf model has a long sock
on its **front right leg** (the Wilf's own right; +X, since models face
−Z), running in its light paw colour from the foot up to the body. The
front left leg has only a short paw at the foot. This applies to every Wilf
model, pet or enemy.

Concept sheets (Codex, 2026-10-23) are in `assets/concepts/pets/wilfs/`, one
per Wilf (front, three-quarter, side, back, an action pose, in-game size,
palette), and `contact_sheet.png` shows all ten.

## Where they hatch

Each Wilf hatches from its own area's egg (`PROGRESSION.md`: one egg per
area). Its rarity there is *open*. The suggestion is that each area's Wilf is
that egg's Legendary, as the Ember Wilf is in the Mossy Glade, so every egg
has a Wilf to chase and a full set means clearing the whole game.

## Open decisions (the owner's)

1. **Each Wilf's rarity** in its egg (suggested: Legendary everywhere).
2. **A set bonus** for owning all ten, or none.
3. **Which Wilf goes to 3D first.** Since all ten share a silhouette, the
   first model is the template for the rest.
