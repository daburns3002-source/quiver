# Windswept Cliffs pets: Claude Design prompts (2026-11-27)

One new Design project per pet, its Codex sheet attached. Opus 5.5, except
the Zephyr Whale on Fable 5.2 (owner, 2026-11-26). Check front, side and 3/4
against the sheet before asking for clips (Idle, Follow, Attack; Root never
moves). The Whistle Marmot, Cliff Puffin and Woolly Alpaca were prompted the
same way on 2026-11-26; the Gale Wilf is Tripo, from the Glowcap Wilf
recoloured.

## 4. Gale Glider (Rare) -> `gale_glider.glb`
Attach: `assets/concepts/blocky/pets/windswept/04_gale_glider_views.png`
```
Roblox pet in blocky voxel style, built from stepped cubes like Steal an Egg / Minecraft, no round shapes.

Gale Glider: a cute flying squirrel pet of a windswept cliff world, a Rare pet. Gliding level, all four legs stretched out to the sides.
A big cube head on a small chubby body, not a box body. Square gliding flaps of skin stretched between each front and back leg, the widest part of the silhouette, with a stepped edge. A big fluffy tail curling up behind, grey with a sky-blue tip.
Pale grey fur with lighter top edges and darker grey round ears, sky-blue flaps, cream cheeks, chest and paws.
Big dark blue-grey eyes with white shines, a tiny sky-blue nose, a small smile.
No clipping: no part pokes through another, every piece sits flush on what it touches, no two faces share a plane. Low poly, under 10k triangles. Match the attached reference image.
Show front, side and 3/4 view on a plain background.
```
